About Kevin

41 years of craftsmanship, still on every job site.

At Kevin Johnson Painting Inc., our story is built on over four decades of hard work, dedication, and a passion for quality craftsmanship.

Kevin Johnson began his painting career in 1985, learning that exceptional painting is about much more than applying paint — it's about proper preparation, attention to detail, and treating every home and business with the care and respect it deserves. Throughout the years, Kevin developed his skills by working on a wide variety of residential and commercial projects, earning a reputation for reliability, professionalism, and outstanding workmanship.

After more than two decades of experience in the trade, Kevin founded Kevin Johnson Painting Inc. in 2008 with a vision of providing customers with honest service, superior craftsmanship, and lasting results. That same commitment continues to guide the company today.

With 41 years of experience, Kevin Johnson Painting Inc. has become a trusted name for homeowners, property managers, and businesses throughout the Bay Area. We specialize in interior and exterior painting, detailed surface preparation, cabinet refinishing, trim and finish work, and protective coatings. Every project is completed using premium products, proven application techniques, and meticulous attention to detail to ensure a beautiful, long-lasting finish.

We understand that your home or business is one of your most valuable investments. That's why we believe in clear communication, dependable service, fair pricing, and delivering results that exceed expectations. From the initial consultation to the final walkthrough, our goal is to make every project a smooth and enjoyable experience.

At Kevin Johnson Painting Inc., we don't just paint properties — we build lasting relationships through integrity, quality, and a commitment to excellence. We are proud of the reputation we've earned over the past 41 years and look forward to serving our community for many years to come.
